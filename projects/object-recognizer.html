🖼️ Object Recognizer: VGG16 Transfer Learning

Swapping a from-scratch CNN for transfer learning, and measuring the difference.

TensorFlowKerasVGG16Transfer LearningOpenCV

Overview

An image classifier that benchmarks transfer learning against a from-scratch CNN baseline, using a pre-trained VGG16 backbone.

Problem

A basic CNN trained from scratch converged slowly and underperformed on the target image dataset, motivating a transfer-learning approach instead of more architecture tweaking.

Approach

Used VGG16 pretrained on ImageNet as a frozen feature extractor, replaced its top layers with a custom dense head trained on the target dataset, then selectively unfroze and fine-tuned deeper VGG16 layers to recover additional accuracy.

Results

  • Faster convergence and better accuracy than the from-scratch CNN baseline
  • Accuracy and loss tracked across frozen-extractor and fine-tuned phases
