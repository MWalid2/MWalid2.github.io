Reinforcement Learning / Energy Systems

🔋 Deep RL for Smart Home Energy Management

Comparing MPC, rule-based control, and Deep RL for scheduling home energy loads.

PythonDeep RLTD3Model Predictive ControlEnergy Systems

Overview

Reproduces and extends an academic Smart Home Energy Management System (SHEMS) study, adapting it to a building with a separate HVAC unit and water heater instead of a single shared heat pump. Grew out of a Data Science Research Intern role at Green Energy Park (Ben Guerir, Morocco), where a residential PV, battery, and controllable-load system was first modeled as a Mixed-Integer Linear Program before being extended into this multi-controller, multi-climate study.

Problem

Managing HVAC and water-heating loads against variable solar generation, occupant comfort, and (in some scenarios) net-metering rules is a sequential decision problem that simple heuristics handle poorly at scale, and that problem looks different depending on climate.

Approach

Implemented and benchmarked three controllers on the same environment: a Model Predictive Control (MPC) optimizer, a rule-based heuristic controller, and a Deep Reinforcement Learning agent trained with TD3 (Twin Delayed DDPG). Evaluated across two geographic sites, Lyon (France) and Ben Guerir (Morocco), and two building types (residential and commercial), including a net-metering regulatory scenario, to compare how each controller performs under two different climatic conditions.

Results

  • Full reproduction of the reference MPC and rule-based baselines
  • TD3 agent trained end to end on combined HVAC and water-heater scheduling
  • Comparative evaluation across 2 sites, 2 building types, and 3 controllers
  • Documented physics formulations and algorithm details for reproducibility
