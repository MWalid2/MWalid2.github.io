💸 Credit Card Fraud Detection

Catching 492 frauds in 284,807 transactions without drowning in false positives.

Pythonimbalanced-learnSMOTEXGBoostPR-AUC

Overview

A fraud-detection classifier on a real anonymized credit-card transaction dataset, where fraud accounts for just 0.172% of all transactions.

Problem

With only 492 frauds among 284,807 transactions, a model that never predicts fraud already scores 99.8% accuracy, so accuracy is actively misleading and the real challenge is precision and recall on the rare class.

Approach

Benchmarked Logistic Regression, SVC, Random Forest, XGBoost, Linear Discriminant Analysis, and Bernoulli Naive Bayes inside imbalanced-learn pipelines, testing SMOTE oversampling, random oversampling, and random and NearMiss undersampling against the raw class distribution, using the 28 PCA-transformed features plus Time and Amount.

Results

  • Evaluation on Precision-Recall AUC instead of accuracy, appropriate for a 0.172% positive rate
  • Systematic comparison of 4 resampling strategies across 6 classifiers
