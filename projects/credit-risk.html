Classification

💳 German Credit Risk Scoring

Scoring loan applicants as good or bad credit risk against an imbalanced baseline.

Pythonscikit-learnXGBoostFeature EncodingPCA

Overview

A credit-risk classifier built on the classic German Credit dataset: 1,000 loan applicants labeled as good or bad credit risk.

Problem

The dataset is imbalanced (700 good vs. 300 bad, a 70/30 split), so a model that always predicts "good" already scores 70% accuracy, meaning accuracy alone cannot be trusted to judge performance.

Approach

Built a preprocessing pipeline handling mixed numeric (age, credit amount, duration) and categorical (job, housing, savings, purpose) features, with missing-value handling for savings and checking accounts. Compared Robust, Standard, and MinMax scaling, ordinal and target encoding, and PCA for dimensionality reduction, feeding Logistic Regression, KNN, SVM, Decision Trees, and XGBoost, with clustering (KMeans, Gaussian Mixture) explored for structure in the data.

Results

  • Evaluation centered on AUC-ROC and F1 score rather than raw accuracy, given the 70/30 baseline
  • Full comparison across 5 classifiers with 3 encoding and scaling strategies
