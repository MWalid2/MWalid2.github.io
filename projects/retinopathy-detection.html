Diabetic Retinopathy Detection

Staging diabetic retinopathy severity from retinal photographs with a fine-tuned ResNet50.

TensorFlowKerasResNet50CNNMedical Imaging

Overview

A CNN-based classifier that stages diabetic retinopathy severity from retinal fundus photographs, trained on the APTOS 2019 Blindness Detection dataset, aimed at supporting earlier screening.

Problem

Diabetic retinopathy damages retinal blood vessels and can cause blindness if untreated, but manual screening of fundus images is slow and doesn't scale to population-level screening.

Approach

  1. Preprocess fundus imagesApply Gaussian blur and a circular crop to normalize lighting variation and remove irrelevant background outside the retina.
  2. Augment the training setRotation, zoom, and shift augmentation to compensate for the limited size typical of medical-imaging datasets.
  3. Warm-start on a frozen ResNet50Pretrain just a new classification head on top of ImageNet-pretrained ResNet50 features, with the backbone frozen.
  4. Fine-tune the full networkUnfreeze ResNet50's layers and continue training at a low learning rate, with early stopping and learning-rate reduction callbacks.
  5. Evaluate on held-out APTOS dataScore accuracy and Cohen's Kappa across the five severity stages (No DR, Mild, Moderate, Severe, Proliferative DR), with confusion matrices and ROC curves for per-class inspection.
  6. Ship a GUIA lightweight interface for uploading a fundus image and getting a stage prediction.

Results

SplitAccuracyCohen's Kappa
Train87.5%0.894
Test81.4%0.855

The train-test gap is modest, about 6 points of accuracy and 0.04 of Kappa, a sign the fine-tuned model generalizes reasonably rather than memorizing the training set, and a Kappa of 0.855 on a 5-class ordinal severity scale indicates strong agreement with the ground-truth grading, not just high raw accuracy.
