👁️ Diabetic Retinopathy Detection

Classifying retinal images into five diabetic retinopathy severity stages.

TensorFlowKerasResNet50CNNMedical Imaging

Overview

A CNN-based classifier that stages diabetic retinopathy severity from retinal fundus photographs, aimed at supporting earlier screening.

Problem

Diabetic retinopathy damages retinal blood vessels and can cause blindness if untreated, but manual screening of fundus images is slow and does not scale to population-level screening.

Approach

Fine-tuned a ResNet50 convolutional network (TensorFlow/Keras) on the APTOS 2019 Blindness Detection dataset, classifying images into five severity stages: No DR, Mild, Moderate, Severe, and Proliferative DR. Applied resizing and augmentation during preprocessing, and used learning-rate scheduling with early stopping during training.

Results

  • Evaluated with accuracy, precision, recall, F1, and Cohen's Kappa
  • Confusion matrices and ROC curves used to inspect per-class performance
