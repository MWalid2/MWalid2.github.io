Industrial Reliability & Maintenance Optimization

Statistical reliability study of a 1,000-unit industrial server fleet. Fitted a Weibull failure distribution (β≈2.97, η≈3,958h), validated it with chi-squared / Kolmogorov–Smirnov tests, then used Barlow–Proschan cost modeling and Markov chain simulation to find the optimal preventive-maintenance interval.

📉 1,485h optimal replacement interval → 63.6% cost reduction vs. corrective-only maintenance.
