Industrial Reliability & Maintenance Optimization

Statistical reliability study of a 1,000-unit industrial server fleet. Fitted a Weibull failure distribution (β≈2.97, η≈3,958h), validated it with chi-squared / Kolmogorov-Smirnov tests, then used Barlow-Proschan cost modeling and Markov chain simulation to find the 1,485h replacement interval that cuts maintenance cost by 63.6% versus corrective-only maintenance.

RSA Broadcast Attacks: Håstad, Coppersmith & Miller-Rabin

Breaks textbook RSA with zero private keys when the same message is broadcast to multiple recipients: Chinese Remainder Theorem plus Håstad's attack for unpadded messages, extended to linearly padded ones via Coppersmith's small-root method and LLL lattice reduction. Includes a Miller-Rabin primality analysis and a Newton-vs-binary-search root-extraction benchmark (10×–100× speedup).
